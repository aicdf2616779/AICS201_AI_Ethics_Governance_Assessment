
# SecureFinance Africa
# AI Governance and Risk Management Framework

## 1. Introduction

SecureFinance Africa requires an AI governance framework to
ensure that its identity verification, fraud detection and
financial risk assessment platform operates responsibly.

This framework adopts the NIST AI Risk Management Framework
(AI RMF 1.0).

## 2. Governance Structure

### Board Risk Committee
- Provides strategic oversight.
- Approves AI risk appetite.
- Reviews significant AI incidents.
- Determines whether deployment conditions are satisfied.

### Chief AI Officer
- Coordinates AI governance.
- Oversees model development and validation.
- Ensures fairness and transparency controls.

### Data Protection Officer
- Oversees personal data protection.
- Advises on privacy impact assessments.
- Monitors data subject rights and privacy risks.

### Chief Information Security Officer
- Oversees AI infrastructure security.
- Implements access controls and security monitoring.
- Coordinates security incident response.

### Legal and Compliance Officer
- Monitors applicable legal requirements.
- Reviews regulatory obligations.
- Maintains compliance documentation.

### Model Risk Management Team
- Independently validates model performance.
- Reviews fairness measurements.
- Investigates model drift and unexplained outcomes.

### Human Review Officers
- Review disputed or high-impact decisions.
- Investigate suspicious fraud alerts.
- Document overrides and escalation decisions.

## 3. NIST AI RMF Application

### 3.1 GOVERN

Establish policies, accountability, organisational culture,
documentation requirements and oversight responsibilities.

### 3.2 MAP

Identify:
- AI system purpose and intended use.
- Stakeholders and affected individuals.
- Data sources and processing activities.
- Ethical, legal and operational risks.
- Potential consequences of incorrect decisions.

### 3.3 MEASURE

Evaluate:
- Demographic parity.
- Disparate impact indicators.
- Equal opportunity.
- Model performance.
- Explainability.
- Privacy and security controls.
- Operational incidents.

### 3.4 MANAGE

Implement:
- Bias mitigation.
- Human review.
- Access controls.
- Privacy safeguards.
- Monitoring and model reassessment.
- Corrective action and incident escalation.

## 4. Human Oversight

Human review must be available for significant adverse
financial decisions and disputed fraud alerts.

Review officers must have sufficient information,
authority and competence to challenge AI outputs.

Human oversight must be meaningful rather than a
rubber-stamping exercise.

## 5. Monitoring and Review

The organisation should monitor:

- Group-based approval rates.
- False positive and false negative rates.
- Model performance.
- Changes in input data.
- Customer complaints.
- Security incidents.
- Human override rates.

Monitoring thresholds must be approved and documented.

## 6. Incident Escalation

1. Detect and record the incident.
2. Notify the responsible operational team.
3. Assess severity and potential customer harm.
4. Escalate significant incidents to the AI governance
   committee and relevant compliance personnel.
5. Suspend affected functionality where necessary.
6. Implement corrective measures.
7. Document findings and lessons learned.

Where a personal data breach occurs, applicable notification
obligations and deadlines under the relevant law must be assessed.

## 7. Deployment Governance

Deployment must remain subject to:
- Independent model validation.
- Fairness remediation.
- Privacy and legal review.
- Effective human oversight.
- Security testing.
- Documented management approval.

## 8. Conclusion

SecureFinance Africa should adopt a risk-based governance
approach that makes accountability, transparency, fairness,
privacy and human oversight central to AI deployment.
